# AI Evaluation Samples

Side-by-side evaluations of AI assistant answers to computer science questions, each scored against a written rubric.

By Oseghale Caleb Iredia, Computer Science graduate (Federal University of Technology, Minna, 2026) and AI data annotator.

## Method

For each question, I:

1. Ask two AI assistants the same question and record their answers exactly, with the tool, model and date.
2. Check every claim: I run the code, work calculations out by hand, and check facts against official sources.
3. Score both answers from 1 to 5 on correctness, completeness, reasoning, safety and clarity (see [RUBRIC.md](RUBRIC.md)).
4. Choose the better answer and explain the deciding difference.

Every evaluation follows [TEMPLATE.md](TEMPLATE.md). The scores and explanations are my own work.
